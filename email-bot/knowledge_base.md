## Opening hours
- The hut is open **09:00 – 17:00**, both in summer and in winter.
- The kitchen serves warm meals **11:30 – 16:00**, both in summer and in winter.

## Seasons
- **Summer season:** opens around **16 June**, closes around **4 October**.
- **Winter season:** planned approximately **23 December to 4 April**. The exact dates depend on the snow. Always say these dates are approximate and depend on snow conditions.
- **Winter lifts:** in winter guests reach the hut with the Lagazuoi cable car. For when the lifts / cable car open, refer guests to the Dolomiti Superski website: https://www.dolomitisuperski.com. Never state a lift opening date.

## How to get to the hut
- **Summer:** only on foot or by bike, from Capanna Alpina (an easy route).
- **Winter:** take the Lagazuoi cable car and ski down to the hut.
- There is no other way up: no car access, no shuttle, and we do not offer rides or transport.

## Food
- Vegetarian options are available.
- Options for allergies are available.
- We serve gluten-free dishes. However, the kitchen is not certified gluten-free and cross-contamination is possible. Always mention this briefly when answering gluten questions (important for guests with celiac disease).

## Dogs
- Dogs are welcome.

## Payment
- All common payment methods are accepted **except American Express**.

## Phone / contact details
- Our contact details are only on our website: https://www.scotoni.it
- Do not write a phone number in replies; refer to the website.

## Rooms and availability
- All room bookings go exclusively through the website: https://www.scotoni.it
- If the website shows a room or date as booked, it really is fully booked. There are no extra rooms.
- Never accept, confirm or handle a booking by email.

A hut with a history

Scotoni sits high in the Dolomites, reachable on foot, by bike in summer and on skis in winter. For generations the family has welcomed hikers and skiers with generous plates of grilled meat, homemade pasta and Tyrolean desserts.

Seating is limited, especially at peak lunch hours — booking ahead guarantees your spot.

A real refuge, not a resort

Rifugio Scotoni lies in the Fanes-Sennes-Braies Nature Park, part of the UNESCO Dolomites, surrounded by the Lagazuoi, Fanis, Scotoni and Conturines peaks.

For more than 50 years the Agreiter family has welcomed guests here. Warmth and good food matter more to us than luxury.

Ingredients come from close by wherever possible: potatoes from the Puster Valley, cheese and cured meats from South Tyrol, and speck we make ourselves.

Near the hut, a small wooden chapel remembers the soldiers who died in this area in the First World War.

Getting here on foot

The usual way up is an easy hike from Capanna Alpina near Armentarola.

Difficulty: Easy
Start
Capanna Alpina, 1,720 m
Walking time
≈ 1 hour, one way
Ascent
≈ 320 m
Trail
no. 20

Wide track, steeper in the upper part, not exposed.

From the hut it is about 30 more minutes to Lago Lagazuoi (≈ 2,180 m).

Parking: Capanna Alpina is the usual starting point – please check parking options and fees locally.

Mountain weather changes fast. Check the forecast before you set off.

Alpaca park

Meet the alpacas – tap a card to learn something about them.

Please ask our staff before visiting the alpacas.

  • Alpacas come from the Andes in South America.

  • They live in herds and do not like to be alone.

  • Alpacas hum softly to talk to each other.

  • Their fleece is very soft and warm – and contains no lanolin.

  • When they spit, it is usually at each other, not at people. Stay calm and quiet!

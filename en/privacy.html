Privacy notice

Draft – to be reviewed by the hut before going live.

Who is responsible

Rifugio Scotoni, Alpe Lagazuoi 2, San Cassiano, Alta Badia, South Tyrol, info@scotoni.it.

What we store

For a table booking: your name, email, phone number, the number of people, optional notes (for example allergies) and the booking details. For a room enquiry: what you write in the form. Nothing else.

Why

Only to manage your booking or answer your enquiry: confirming, changing or cancelling it, and contacting you about it (for example if the weather closes the terrace).

How long

Booking data is anonymised {months} months after the booking date. You can ask us to delete it earlier at any time.

Cookies and tracking

This website uses no cookies, no analytics and no third-party services. Your language and theme choice are saved only in your own browser.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your data by writing to info@scotoni.it.
